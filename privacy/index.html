Privacy

Privacy notice

This website is for information only. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Apart from the research page described below, it has no forms and we do not collect personal information through it.

If you contact us

If you write to us or call us, we use your name, contact details and message only to reply to you and to continue the conversation you started. We do not sell your information or share it with others, except where the law requires us to.

Research page

If you take part in research through a page on this site, it sends us your name, mobile number, bank and the text you paste, with every name hidden and every number replaced by 9. This is stored in Google Sheets. We use your name and number only to check your answer and arrange your payment, then delete them. The masked text is kept separately, without your name or number, for the research.

Typefaces

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser asks Google's servers for those files, and Google receives your device's IP address in the process.
